# Acme Semiconductor -- Q3 FY26 Results (unaudited)

Total revenue was 1,480 million dollars, compared with 1,310 million in the
prior-year quarter. Data Center segment revenue was 850 million dollars, up
from 790 million a year ago. Client segment revenue was 430 million dollars.
Embedded segment revenue was 200 million dollars.

For the full year, management guided to total revenue of 5,600 million
dollars. Analysts had modeled 5,410 million. Operating income was 296
million dollars against 240 million in the prior-year quarter.
