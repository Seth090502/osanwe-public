# Porting brief: tidy-notes

The tidy-notes hook keeps the notes in the vault tidy. It should run at a sensible
point in the session and clean up anything that looks messy or out of place, using
good judgment about what "messy" means in context. Port it however your harness
prefers -- the important thing is that notes end up tidier than they started.
